=== the model's own limit, on good hardware ===

The document the phone produced reads:

  Here is the revised document:
  **Title:** Offline Release Checklist

The model's preamble ("Here is the revised document:") is inside the document body. The 0.5B model
needed three prompt designs to produce a document at all (evidence/e2-model-protocol.txt) and still
leaks framing into the artifact. Faster hardware does not fix that: it is a capability limit of the
0.5B model, and it is the cost the demonstration carries.
