=== bridge compilation is not possible on this machine ===
The AAR's public surface is Kotlin suspend functions returning kotlin.Unit / Continuation
(javap of com.microsoft.foundrylocal.api.FoundryLocalManager and .Catalog). It bundles no
kotlin-stdlib, and this machine has no Kotlin compiler and no Gradle:
  $ command -v kotlinc gradle
  (neither resolves)
So the Kotlin facade in DECISION-bridge.md §3.1 cannot be compiled here without installing a
Kotlin toolchain (kotlin-compiler plus kotlin-stdlib from Maven Central). That was not
approved and was not done.
